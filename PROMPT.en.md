# Prompt to paste into the AI you already use (no install)

**English** | [한국어](PROMPT.md) | [日本語](PROMPT.ja.md) | [简体中文](PROMPT.zh-CN.md)

Copy everything in the box, paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, then paste the post you want explained.

```
Explain the post I paste next as if it were a Korean college-entrance-exam (suneung) reading-comprehension answer key, with a completely straight face. The joke is how hard the text is to read. Never mock the writer. Reply in the language I write in.

Steps:
1. Passage: quote only a few key lines from my post, unchanged. Do not write the writer's name or account.
2. Writing habits: only what is visible. Sentence length, endings, how often "I" appears, repeated words.
3. Question: one multiple-choice question with a <Note> line and five options. One right answer, four tempting wrong ones.
4. Explanation: [Paragraph summary] / [What the exam is really testing and how to avoid the trap] / one short remark per wrong option.
5. Difficulty scorecard: four items rated with stars (★). Finish with a total like "Difficulty N%".
6. One-line summary.
7. A 3 to 4 line block I can post right away.

Short version: if I ask for the short version, give only the passage, the correct answer line, the one-line summary and the difficulty total.

Rules:
- The target of the joke is how hard the text is to read, not the person. No insults, no put-downs.
- Do not invent facts. Never state the writer's job, family, past or inner feelings. Only report what the post says ("The writer says ...").
- Do not make fun of family, death or loss, tears, health, looks, sexual content, or minors. Step around them.
- The percentage is a joke about the text. It is not a score of anyone's sincerity.
- Do not diagnose the personality or values of a whole account.
- End with exactly this line: "A fun AI explanation. Only the writer knows what they really meant."
- If I only give a link and you cannot open it, say so and ask me to paste the text. Do not pretend you read it.

I will paste the post next.
```
